The product

What is Ojas Hair Oil?

An Ayurvedic hair oil made from methi (fenugreek), onion, flax seed and mustard oil. It is a pre-wash oil for the scalp and lengths — you massage it in, leave it, and wash it out.

What exactly is in it?

Methi (Trigonella foenum-graecum), onion (Allium cepa), flax seed (Linum usitatissimum) and mustard oil as the carrier base. The full ingredient list is printed on the label — we don't hide anything behind a "proprietary blend".

Using it

How do I use it?

Warm a little oil. Massage into the scalp for 3–5 minutes, comb it through the lengths, leave for about 30 minutes, then wash out. Use 2–3 times a week.

Can I leave it overnight?

You can, and many people in India traditionally do. Thirty minutes is enough to condition; overnight is preference, not necessity. Use a pillow cover you don't mind oiling.

Should I patch-test?

Yes. Apply a small amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours before first use. Mustard oil and onion can irritate sensitive skin. Stop using it if irritation appears, and don't apply to broken skin.

Can I use it if I'm pregnant, or on a child?

Please ask your doctor first. We are not able to give medical advice, and we would rather say that than guess.

What it does — and doesn't

This is the section most brands are vague about, so we've made it the most specific.
Will this regrow my hair or stop hair fall?

No. Ojas Hair Oil is a cosmetic product, not a medicine. It does not regrow hair, stop hair fall, treat hair loss, reverse greying, or treat dandruff or any other condition — and we won't suggest otherwise to make a sale.

If you are losing hair in a way that worries you, that is a conversation for a doctor or dermatologist, not an oil.

So what does it actually do?

Used consistently, it nourishes and comforts the scalp, conditions dry and rough lengths for softness and shine, helps hair look fuller and healthier, and helps reduce the look of breakage.

How soon will I see a difference?

Softness and shine tend to show early. Anything to do with how hair looks over time is a matter of consistent use over weeks, not days. Ayurveda is a ritual, not an overnight promise — and results vary with hair type, routine and consistency.

Is it "clinically proven"?

No, and we won't print that phrase without a study behind it. What we can tell you is exactly what is in the bottle and what it is for.
