# Ojas — Email/WhatsApp Flows (Klaviyo-ready copy)

_Compliant copy for the core automations. Build these as Klaviyo flows; use merge tags for name/product. Keep claims in support/appearance framing._

## Flow 1 — Welcome / Quiz result (triggered on Lead)
**Email 1 (immediately): "Your Ojas routine"**
- Subject: Your personalised Ojas routine, [First name] 🌿
- Body: Here's the routine we matched to your hair. Recap their recommendation. Soft CTA to shop. Reassure: honest Ayurveda, no miracle promises.

**Email 2 (+1 day): "How to actually use it"**
- Subject: The 30-minute ritual most people get wrong
- Body: The step-by-step ritual (warm → massage → comb → leave → wash). Builds trust + value.

**Email 3 (+3 days): "A first-order offer"**
- Subject: A little something to start your ritual
- Body: Present [OFFER] with a clear CTA. Light urgency (expires in X days).

## Flow 2 — Abandoned cart (triggered on AddToCart/checkout start, no purchase)
**Email 1 (+1 hour):** Subject: You left your Ojas routine behind — gentle nudge + product recap + CTA.
**Email 2 (+24 hours):** Subject: Still thinking it over? — address a common question (natural? how to use?) + CTA. Optional small incentive.

## Flow 3 — Post-purchase (triggered on Purchase)
**Email 1 (on ship):** Subject: Your Ojas ritual is on its way — tracking + how-to-use reminder.
**Email 2 (+10 days):** Subject: How's the ritual going? — usage tips + ask for a review + subscribe-and-save.

## Flow 4 — Winback (no purchase in [60] days)
**Email 1:** Subject: It's been a while — we saved your routine — recap + a reason to return.
