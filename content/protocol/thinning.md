# Your protocol: thinner-looking hair

For hair that's started to feel or look thinner than before. This guide is
about consistent scalp care to support a fuller-looking appearance over
time — not a regrowth promise. Ayurveda is a ritual, not an overnight fix.

## Understanding thinner-looking hair

Hair can look thinner for reasons ranging from styling habits to shedding
that's part of a normal hair cycle to simple buildup weighing strands
down. This protocol focuses on what a consistent scalp-care ritual can
support: a comfortable, clarified scalp and hair that looks and feels
fuller — not on diagnosing or treating an underlying cause.

## Ingredients & why

- **Mustard oil** — a traditional Ayurvedic base oil; warms easily for
  massage and helps carry the other ingredients into the scalp.
- **Onion** — used in Ayurvedic and folk hair-care traditions as a
  scalp-nourishing ingredient in oil infusions.
- **Garlic** — used in small amounts alongside onion for the same
  scalp-nourishing tradition; strong-smelling, so rinse thoroughly.
- **Flax seed** — adds slip and conditioning to the oil blend, making it
  easier to massage in and comb through without pulling.

## 4-week ritual calendar

- **Week 1** — Twice this week: warm 1–2 tsp oil, massage into the scalp
  for 3–5 minutes using fingertips (not nails), leave 30 minutes, then
  wash. Focus on getting comfortable with the massage technique.
- **Week 2** — Same twice-weekly oiling, extend the massage to 5 minutes
  and add gentle strokes from crown outward. Start noting how your scalp
  feels day-to-day (tight, comfortable, oily) in a quick note.
- **Week 3** — Move to 3x/week if your scalp tolerates it well (not oily
  or irritated). Keep the massage consistent — this is a ritual, not a
  race.
- **Week 4** — Settle into whatever frequency (2–3x/week) felt best in
  weeks 1–3. This becomes your ongoing routine, not a one-off treatment.

## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** massage gently with fingertips — pulling or scratching irritates
  the scalp.
- **Do** rinse thoroughly after garlic-containing oil; residual smell
  means residue is still there.
- **Do** stay consistent — twice or three times a week, every week, works
  better than an intense single session.
- **Don't** expect an overnight change — a fuller-looking appearance from
  a consistent ritual builds over weeks, not days.
- **Don't** massage a scalp that's actively irritated, broken, or
  freshly colored — wait until it's calm.

## Troubleshooting

- **Scalp feels oilier than before** — you're likely over-applying; drop
  to 1 tsp and once a week until it settles.
- **No visible change after 4 weeks** — that's normal; give it 8–12 weeks
  of consistent ritual before judging, and revisit whether your massage
  technique and frequency matched the calendar above.
- **Smell lingers after washing** — double-rinse and consider a
  clarifying wash once every couple of weeks.

---
Not a medicine. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any
disease. This protocol supports a fuller-looking, healthier-looking
appearance through a consistent ritual — it does not regrow hair or stop
hair fall.
