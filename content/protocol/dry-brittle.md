# Your protocol: dry, rough or brittle hair

For hair and scalp that feel dry, rough, or brittle to the touch. This
guide focuses on conditioning and softness — restoring moisture and
smoothness with a consistent ritual.

## Understanding dry, brittle hair

Dryness usually means the hair's natural moisture and oils have been
stripped faster than they're replenished — from washing frequency, heat
styling, weather, or hard water. This protocol is about replacing what's
being stripped: conditioning the lengths and comforting a dry scalp.

## Ingredients & why

- **Mustard oil** — an emollient base oil that coats and softens the hair
  shaft, helping reduce the rough, dry feel.
- **Flax seed** — mucilage-rich; adds slip and a conditioning, softening
  effect, especially useful for detangling dry, rough lengths.
- **Onion** — included for scalp comfort in the traditional blend, used
  here in smaller proportion than in the thinning protocol since dry
  scalps benefit more from moisture than from a strong active ingredient.
- **Garlic** — light amount for scalp comfort; if your scalp is
  sensitive/dry, you can skip garlic entirely and use a mustard-oil +
  flaxseed blend on its own.

## 4-week ritual calendar

- **Week 1** — Oil the lengths (not just scalp) twice this week: warm oil,
  apply from mid-length to ends, gently massage the scalp for 3 minutes,
  leave 45 minutes to an hour (longer than the standard 30 for extra dry
  hair), then wash with a gentle, non-stripping wash.
- **Week 2** — Same twice-weekly ritual. Add a pre-wash detangle: comb
  through gently *before* washing, while the oil is still in, to reduce
  breakage from tangled, dry strands.
- **Week 3** — If hair still feels rough, increase leave-in time to
  overnight once this week (oil in before bed, wash in the morning).
  Continue twice-weekly base routine otherwise.
- **Week 4** — Settle into a sustainable rhythm — most dry-hair routines
  land at 2x/week oiling with longer leave-in time as the ongoing ritual.

## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** apply from mid-lengths to ends generously — dry hair needs more
  product on the lengths than the scalp.
- **Do** detangle gently, wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends and
  working up.
- **Do** use a gentle, sulfate-light wash — a harsh shampoo undoes the
  conditioning work.
- **Don't** wash daily — this strips moisture faster than the ritual can
  replace it; aim for every 2-3 days if your scalp allows.
- **Don't** use high heat styling on days you've just washed — dry hair
  is more vulnerable right after a wash.

## Troubleshooting

- **Hair still feels rough after 4 weeks** — increase leave-in time
  (try overnight) and check your washing frequency isn't undoing progress.
- **Oil feels heavy/greasy the next day** — you may be over-applying;
  reduce to a lighter application focused on the driest sections only.
- **Scalp feels dry too** — add the 3-minute scalp massage step even on
  weeks you're focused on lengths.
