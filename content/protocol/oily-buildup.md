# Your protocol: oily roots / buildup

For a scalp that feels oily and weighed down, or shows visible buildup
between washes. This guide focuses on clarifying the scalp without
stripping it dry — a different balance than the other protocols.

## Understanding oily roots and buildup

An oily scalp overproduces natural oils, and buildup (product residue,
environmental dirt, dead skin) compounds the weighed-down feeling. The
instinct is often to wash more or use harsher products, which can
backfire — stripping the scalp too hard can trigger *more* oil production
in response. This protocol is about clarifying gently and consistently,
not stripping aggressively.

## Ingredients & why

- **Garlic** — used here as the primary active in the blend; traditionally
  valued for a clarifying effect on the scalp.
- **Onion** — supports the clarifying blend alongside garlic.
- **Mustard oil** — used in a *lighter* quantity than in the other
  protocols — enough to carry the garlic/onion into the scalp without
  adding to the oiliness.
- **Flax seed** — light amount, mainly for ease of application rather than
  heavy conditioning (which an oily scalp doesn't need as much of).

## 4-week ritual calendar

- **Week 1** — Once this week: apply a light amount of oil blend directly
  to the scalp only (not lengths), massage 3 minutes, leave just 15–20
  minutes (shorter than standard — oily scalps don't need long leave-in),
  then wash thoroughly with a clarifying (not heavy/moisturizing) wash.
- **Week 2** — Same once-weekly ritual. Between washes, if roots feel
  weighed down, a quick water-only rinse (no product) can help without
  over-washing with shampoo.
- **Week 3** — If buildup persists, add a clarifying-only wash (no oil
  that week) mid-week, then return to the oil ritual the following week.
- **Week 4** — Settle into once-weekly oil ritual as the ongoing routine —
  more frequent oiling usually makes an oily scalp worse, not better.

## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** apply oil to the scalp only, not the lengths, unless your ends
  are dry despite oily roots (uncommon but possible).
- **Do** keep leave-in time short — 15–20 minutes is enough for a
  clarifying blend to work without adding excess oil.
- **Do** wash thoroughly — buildup won't clear if the wash isn't
  thorough enough to lift both the oil blend and existing residue.
- **Don't** wash daily "to fix the oiliness" — this often triggers more
  oil production; stick to the calendar's rhythm instead.
- **Don't** apply a heavy, long leave-in oil treatment (like the dry-hair
  protocol) — it will make buildup worse.

## Troubleshooting

- **Still oily by day 2-3 after washing** — try shortening leave-in time
  further (10 minutes) and confirm you're rinsing thoroughly.
- **Buildup visible even right after washing** — add the mid-week
  clarifying-only wash from Week 3 as an ongoing addition, not just a
  one-time fix.
- **Scalp feels dry/tight after clarifying wash** — you may be washing
  too aggressively; switch to a gentler clarifying formula and keep the
  once-weekly oil ritual to rebalance.
