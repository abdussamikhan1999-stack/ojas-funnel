# Your protocol: breakage

For hair that breaks noticeably when brushing or washing. This guide
focuses on gentle handling and strengthening the *ritual around* your
hair, not just the oil itself — breakage often comes from a combination of
dryness, rough handling, and fragile strands.

## Understanding breakage

Breakage is different from shedding — it's strands snapping mid-length or
at the ends rather than falling from the root. It's usually made worse by
brushing dry/tangled hair, harsh towel-drying, and heat styling. This
protocol pairs a conditioning oil ritual with handling changes, since the
handling changes matter as much as the oil.

## Ingredients & why

- **Flax seed** — the key ingredient here; its slip helps hair detangle
  more easily, reducing the friction that causes breakage during combing.
- **Mustard oil** — coats and conditions the hair shaft, adding a
  protective layer before brushing or washing.
- **Onion and garlic** — included in smaller proportion, mainly for
  scalp comfort as part of the same ritual; the emphasis in this protocol
  is on the flaxseed's conditioning/slip effect, not these two.

## 4-week ritual calendar

- **Week 1** — Twice this week: apply oil generously to lengths and ends,
  gently finger-detangle *before* combing, leave 30–45 minutes, then wash.
  After washing, blot (don't rub) with a soft towel or an old t-shirt.
- **Week 2** — Same twice-weekly ritual. Add: comb only when hair is damp
  and has a leave-in/conditioner on it, never bone-dry, using a wide-tooth
  comb starting from the ends.
- **Week 3** — Introduce a protective step before bed: a loose braid or
  buff to reduce friction against your pillow overnight, on top of the
  twice-weekly oil ritual.
- **Week 4** — Settle into the combined routine: twice-weekly oil ritual +
  gentle detangling technique + nighttime protection, as your ongoing
  approach rather than a one-time fix.

## Do's and don'ts

- **Do** detangle before washing, while oil is still in the hair — dry,
  tangled hair breaks more under a comb.
- **Do** use a wide-tooth comb on damp hair, working from ends up to
  roots, not roots down.
- **Do** blot hair dry instead of rubbing with a towel.
- **Don't** brush hair when it's completely dry and tangle-free from a
  previous session skipped — always detangle with product/oil present.
- **Don't** skip the nighttime protection step (braid/buff) — friction
  overnight adds up across weeks.

## Troubleshooting

- **Still seeing breakage after 4 weeks** — check whether heat styling or
  tight hairstyles (buns, tight ponytails) are undoing the ritual; those
  are common hidden causes of ongoing breakage.
- **Detangling still painful** — increase oil leave-in time and always
  start detangling from the ends, moving up in small sections.
- **Ends look rough/split despite the ritual** — the ritual conditions and
  helps reduce the *look* of breakage going forward; existing split ends
  need a trim, the protocol won't repair them.
