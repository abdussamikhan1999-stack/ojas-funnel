# Meta ad copy — The Ojas Hair & Scalp Protocol

Filled from `revenue-toolkit/ad-creative-copywriting/templates/ad-copy.md`
using `PRODUCT.md`. Four variants, one hook each, same offer/CTA — test
per `ads-meta/README.md`'s creative-testing matrix (one variable at a
time). All copy stays inside the claims policy in this repo's `CLAUDE.md`:
support/appearance framing only, never regrowth/cure language.

## Variant A — curiosity gap

- **Hook pattern used:** "Nobody tells you [surprising fact]"
- **Primary text:** Nobody tells you that oiling your hair the wrong way
  for your specific scalp is why it's not working. Take the free 2-minute
  quiz, get matched to your exact concern (thinning-looking hair, dryness,
  oily roots, or breakage), and get your personalized Ayurvedic protocol —
  the ritual, the ingredients, the order to do it in. Founding price ₹249
  (usually ₹399).
- **Headline:** Your hair, actually explained
- **Description:** 2-min quiz → your protocol
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Creative:** Split-screen: "guessing" (random bottles) vs. "matched"
  (single labeled protocol) — or a phone screen showing the quiz result
  screen.

## Variant B — named mistake

- **Hook pattern used:** "The [mistake] costing you [cost]"
- **Primary text:** The mistake: using the same hair routine no matter
  what your scalp actually needs. Oily roots and dry lengths don't want
  the same thing. Take the quiz, get matched to your concern, and get a
  protocol built for it — not a generic one. ₹249 founding price, limited
  to the first 200.
- **Headline:** Stop guessing with your hair
- **Description:** Get matched in 2 minutes
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Creative:** Close-up scalp/hair texture shot with a bold text overlay
  of the headline.

- **Hook pattern used:** "[Number] of [Audience] switched to this — here's why"
