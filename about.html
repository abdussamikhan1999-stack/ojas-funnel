About Ojas

Honest Ayurveda, without the false promises.

Ojas began with a simple idea: hair care rooted in Ayurveda that tells you the truth. We start at the scalp, name every ingredient, and are clear about what our oil can and can't do.

Why we started

Walk down any hair-care aisle in India and you will be promised the impossible — regrowth, reversal, a cure for something that was never a disease. Those promises sell, and they are why so many people have stopped believing the category altogether.

We think the honest version is more useful. An oil is an oil. Used consistently, a good one nourishes your scalp, conditions dry lengths and helps hair look fuller and healthier. That is a real, worthwhile thing. It is not a miracle, and we will not dress it up as one.
